# Week 5 - Behavioural diagrams

## Problem

The team modelled actors, actions and responsibilities before implementation.

## Relevant Context

Prepared the use case diagram, identifying students, organisers, administrators and their interactions.

## Key Observation

A request and its approval are separate actions performed by different actors.

## Solution

Keep approval decisions with the organiser and show the resulting system update after the decision.
